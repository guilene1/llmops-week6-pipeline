# Hiring and Offer Approval Matrix

## Purpose

Who must approve a requisition and an offer. Available to managers and above.

## Opening a requisition

Every requisition needs an approved headcount slot. Requisitions without a slot
are rejected by the recruiting team without review.

- L1 to L3: hiring manager plus department head
- L4 to L5: department head plus Finance
- L6 and above: department head, Finance, and the executive sponsor

## Interview panel

A panel has a minimum of 3 interviewers. At least one interviewer must be outside
the hiring team. For L4 and above, at least one interviewer must be at or above
the level being hired.

Panel members submit written feedback within 2 business days of the interview.
Feedback submitted after a hiring decision is recorded but not counted.

## Making an offer

Offers are made by the recruiter, never by the hiring manager directly. The
recruiter receives the approved band for the role from Total Rewards. Managers do
not receive the full band table.

- Offer at or below band midpoint: recruiter and hiring manager
- Offer above band midpoint: add Total Rewards approval
- Any signing bonus above 10,000 dollars: add department head approval

## Internal candidates

Internal candidates must be in role for at least 12 months unless their current
manager agrees in writing. An internal move at the same level does not
automatically carry a salary increase.

## Contractor conversion

Converting a contractor to an employee requires a requisition, a full interview
loop, and a 30 day gap check with Legal for co-employment risk.

## Time to respond

Candidates receive a decision within 5 business days of their final interview. A
delay beyond 10 business days requires the recruiter to inform the candidate in
writing.
