# Glossary

**Accrual** - the rate at which paid time off builds up. At Northwind, time off is granted in
full at the start of the year rather than accrued monthly, except in your first year.

**Core hours** - 10:00 to 15:00 local time, when you are expected to be reachable.

**Exempt and non-exempt** - a United States classification under the Fair Labor Standards Act.
Non-exempt employees are entitled to overtime pay. This distinction does not exist in the UK,
German or Indian entities.

**FTE** - full-time equivalent. A 0.6 FTE employee works three days a week and receives
pro-rated time off and benefits.

**Garden leave** - a period during notice when you remain employed and paid but do not work.
Used occasionally in the UK entity.

**Open enrolment** - the November window when you may change benefit elections for the
following year.

**PTO** - Paid Time Off. The same thing this handbook calls paid time off or holiday: the
25 days a year you book in Workday, separate from public holidays. See the Time Off Policy
(NW-POL-010).

**FTE** and **PTO** are the two abbreviations people use most in tickets to the service desk.

**PIP** - Performance Improvement Plan. Note that the United States medical plan documents
use PIP to mean Personal Injury Protection, which is unrelated.

**Probation** - an initial period during which either side may end employment with shorter
notice. Length varies by country.

**Works council** - the elected employee representative body in the German entity, which must
be consulted on certain changes.
