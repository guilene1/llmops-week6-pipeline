# Commitment

Northwind Systems makes every employment decision on merit. We do not discriminate on the
basis of race, colour, national origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity,
age, disability, marital status, pregnancy, veteran status, or any other characteristic
protected by applicable law.

This applies to hiring, pay, promotion, training, discipline and termination.

## Reasonable adjustments

If you need an adjustment to do your job, contact People Operations at
hr-help@northwind.example. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis. We will discuss what
would help and respond within 10 working days.

Common adjustments include assistive software, modified hours, additional breaks, a different
desk location, and changes to how meetings run.

## Recruitment

Every interview panel of three or more people must include at least one member from a
different team than the hiring manager. Structured scorecards are required for all roles.

## If something goes wrong

Report concerns to your manager, People Operations, or the anonymous ethics line. All reports
are investigated. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is itself a disciplinary
matter.
