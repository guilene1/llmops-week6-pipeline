Dress code

Northwind has no formal dress code. Dress in a way that is comfortable for you and
appropriate for what your day holds.

If you are meeting customers, regulators or candidates, dress to the standard that meeting
would expect. Your manager will tell you if there is a specific expectation.

Safety requirements in the Bangalore data centre area override personal preference: closed
shoes and no loose jewellery.

Video calls have the same standard as the office. There is no expectation to be on camera.
