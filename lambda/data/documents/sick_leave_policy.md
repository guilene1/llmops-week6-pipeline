# Sick leave

If you are too unwell to work, do not work. Tell your manager as early as you can on the
first day, by whatever means is normal for your team.

## Paid sick leave by entity

- United States: 10 paid sick days per calendar year, non-accruing, resetting on 1 January
- United Kingdom: company sick pay at full salary for up to 8 weeks in any 12-month period,
  then Statutory Sick Pay
- Germany: continued payment of salary (Entgeltfortzahlung) for up to 6 weeks, then
  Krankengeld from the health insurer
- India: 12 days paid sick leave per calendar year

## Medical certificates

A certificate is required from the fourth consecutive working day of absence in the United
States, the United Kingdom and India. In Germany a certificate is required from the fourth
calendar day, and your manager may request one from the first day.

Send certificates to hr-help@northwind.example, not to your manager.

## Long-term absence

Absence beyond 4 consecutive weeks is managed by People Operations, who will agree a contact
schedule with you and, where useful, arrange an occupational health assessment. The aim is a
return to work, with adjustments if they help.

See also Short-Term Disability (NW-POL-035), which covers income replacement beyond company
sick pay in the United States.

## Sickness during booked time off

If you fall ill during booked paid time off and provide a medical certificate, those days are
reclassified as sick leave and returned to your paid time off balance.
