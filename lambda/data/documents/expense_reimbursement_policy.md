# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## What can be claimed

Reasonable business expenses incurred while performing your job. Every claim
needs an itemised receipt. A credit card statement is not a receipt.

## Approval thresholds

- Under 500 dollars: no pre-approval required. Submit after the expense is incurred.
- 500 to 2,000 dollars: written manager pre-approval required before booking or purchase.
- Over 2,000 dollars: manager and department head pre-approval, both in writing.

Splitting a single purchase into smaller claims to stay under a threshold is a
policy violation and is treated as misconduct.

## Submission deadline

Submit claims within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 60 days
are not reimbursed except with department head approval. Claims for a closed
financial year are not reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Payment timing

Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run, provided approval is complete
5 business days before the pay date.

## Meal limits while travelling

- Breakfast: 20 dollars
- Lunch: 30 dollars
- Dinner: 55 dollars

Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment is charged to the client
entertainment code and requires the names of attendees on the claim.

## Not reimbursable

Traffic fines and parking tickets, personal travel extensions, hotel minibar,
in-flight upgrades purchased at your own initiative, and personal subscriptions.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at 0.67 dollars per mile.
Record the start point, end point, and business purpose for each trip.
