# What Northwind provides to work with

## Standard equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, a docking station, a monitor, a keyboard and a mouse. You
choose between the Mac and the Windows option when you join, and again at each refresh.

Laptops are refreshed every three years, or sooner if the machine is genuinely holding you
up. "Genuinely holding you up" is your manager's judgement, not IT's.

You keep the equipment while you are employed and return it when you leave, as set out in the
Offboarding Checklist. Northwind does not sell equipment to departing employees.

## Home office allowance

Employees working from home two or more days a week receive 800 US dollars in their first
year and 300 US dollars each year after, for a desk, a chair, lighting and anything else that
makes home working comfortable.

Items bought with the allowance belong to you, not to Northwind, and you do not return them.
The exception is a second monitor or other IT equipment supplied by IT, which is Northwind
property and does come back.

Claim it through expenses with a receipt. Your manager approves. It cannot be taken as cash,
and unspent allowance does not carry into the next year.

## Ergonomic assessments

Anyone may request an ergonomic assessment, in the office or at home, by contacting
facilities@northwind.example. An assessment is arranged within 10 working days.

Equipment recommended by an assessment is paid for by Northwind separately and does not come
out of the home office allowance. That includes specialist chairs, sit-stand desks, footrests
and input devices.

If you have a health condition or disability that affects how you work, contact People
Operations rather than facilities. Adjustments are handled under the Equal Opportunity and
Non-Discrimination Policy and there is no cost limit on them.

## Mobile phones

Northwind does not provide mobile phones except to employees who are on call. If your role
requires you to be reachable outside working hours, your manager arranges a phone or a monthly
allowance of 40 US dollars.

## Internet at home

Northwind does not pay for home internet, on the basis that most people would have it anyway.
Employees in Germany should note that a portion may be tax deductible; payroll cannot advise
on this and you should ask your own tax adviser.

## Damage, loss and theft

Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT immediately, at any hour, using the number in the IT
Acceptable Use policy. Devices are encrypted and can be wiped remotely.

You are not charged for equipment damaged in normal use, or for a first loss. Repeated losses
are handled as a performance matter rather than a charge.

## Working from somewhere that is not home

Working from another country, even for a week, can create tax and immigration obligations for
you and for Northwind. Read the Remote and Hybrid Working Policy before booking anything, and
tell People Operations at least four weeks ahead.
