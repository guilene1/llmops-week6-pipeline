# Parental leave, United Kingdom

This policy applies to employees of the United Kingdom entity. The United States, German and
Indian entities have separate policies with different entitlements.

## Entitlement

Northwind enhanced maternity and adoption pay is 26 weeks at full pay, followed by 13 weeks
at the statutory rate, followed by 13 weeks unpaid. Total leave is up to 52 weeks.

Enhanced paternity and partner pay is 8 weeks at full pay.

Enhanced pay requires 26 weeks of continuous service by the 15th week before the expected
week of childbirth. Employees with less service receive the statutory entitlement.

## Shared parental leave

Shared parental leave is available and Northwind enhances it to match the maternity provision
above for the period the employee takes. Notice requirements follow the statutory scheme:
8 weeks written notice of each period of leave.

## Keeping in touch days

You may work up to 10 keeping in touch days during leave without ending it, paid at your
normal rate. These are optional on both sides.

## Benefits during leave

Pension contributions continue at the normal employer rate based on your pre-leave salary.
Private medical cover continues. Annual leave continues to accrue for the full period.

## Returning

You return to the same role if your leave was 26 weeks or less. After longer leave you return
to the same role or, if that is not reasonably practicable, a suitable alternative on no less
favourable terms.
