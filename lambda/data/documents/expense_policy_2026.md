# Claiming money back

Northwind pays for what you reasonably need to do your job. The test is whether you would be
comfortable explaining the expense to a colleague. If not, do not claim it.

## Submitting a claim

Claims go through Expensify, linked from Workday. Attach a receipt for anything over 25 US
dollars. Below that, a line in the description is enough.

Submit within 60 days of the spend. Claims older than 60 days need your manager and Finance
to approve, and claims older than 6 months are not paid at all. This is a tax requirement in
several of our locations, not Finance being difficult.

Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run. Cut-off is the 20th of the month.

## Travel: what you may spend

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Hotel, per night, standard cities | 220 US dollars |
| Hotel, per night, New York, London, Berlin | 300 US dollars |
| Meals, per day, with receipts | 75 US dollars |
| Meals, per day, without receipts (per diem) | see Per Diem Rates (NW-POL-042) |
| Ground transport, per journey | 80 US dollars |

Book flights at least 14 days ahead where you can. Economy on flights under six hours;
premium economy is allowed above six hours, and business class needs director approval.

Rail is preferred to flying where the journey is under five hours door to door.

## What is not reimbursed

- Alcohol, except at a client dinner or an approved team event
- Fines, penalties and parking tickets
- Personal entertainment, films and minibar
- Upgrades you choose for comfort rather than need
- Anything for a family member travelling with you

## Client entertainment

Allowed, with your manager's approval in advance, up to 150 US dollars per head. Record who
attended and which company they work for; the gift and hospitality rules in the Code of
Conduct apply to what you spend on other people.

## Working from another Northwind office

Flights and hotel are covered where the trip was asked for. Where you choose to visit another
office for your own reasons, Northwind does not pay, though you are welcome to go.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year may request a corporate card. Card spend is
still claimed in Expensify, coded, and matched against the statement. An unreconciled card
transaction after 60 days is deducted from salary, which nobody enjoys, so reconcile monthly.

## Approvals

Your manager approves up to 2,000 US dollars. Above that, their manager approves as well.
Above 10,000, Finance approves. Nobody approves their own expenses, including executives.

## If a claim is rejected

You will be told why, in Expensify. If you disagree, reply there and it goes back to your
manager, then to Finance. Rejected claims are not a disciplinary matter unless they are
dishonest.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at 0.67 dollars per mile.
Record the start point, end point, and business purpose for each trip.
