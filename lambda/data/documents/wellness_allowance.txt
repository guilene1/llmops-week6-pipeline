Title: Wellness Allowance
Document ID: NW-POL-035
Version: 2026.1
Effective: 2026-01-01
Owner: People Operations
Applies to: All employees after 90 days of service

Wellness allowance

Northwind pays up to 600 US dollars a year, or the local equivalent, towards things that keep
you well. You claim it back through the expenses system like any other expense, with a
receipt.

What it covers, without needing approval:
  - Gym membership, classes, sports clubs and equipment
  - Bicycles, helmets and bicycle servicing
  - Running shoes and sports clothing
  - Meditation and sleep apps and subscriptions
  - Nutrition and dietetics consultations
  - Smoking cessation programmes and products
  - Ergonomic items for home that are not covered by the equipment policy
  - Massage and physiotherapy not covered by medical insurance

What it does not cover:
  - Anything for someone other than you
  - Alcohol, supplements marketed for weight loss, and cosmetic treatments
  - Medical treatment that your insurance would cover
  - Holidays, spa breaks and travel

The allowance runs by calendar year and does not carry over. It is pro-rated for employees
who join partway through the year: joining in July gives you 300 dollars.

Claims are paid with your salary in the month after approval. Your manager approves them, and
managers are asked to approve rather than judge: if it is on the list above, approve it.

Employees in India and Germany should note that the allowance may be treated as a taxable
benefit depending on what it is spent on. Payroll will tell you if that applies.

This allowance is separate from the home office allowance in the Equipment and Home Working
policy, and separate from any medical insurance benefit. You may claim all three.
