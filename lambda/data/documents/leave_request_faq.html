Title: Booking Time Off - Frequently Asked Questions

Document ID: NW-POL-020

Version: 2026.1

Effective: 2026-01-01

Owner: People Operations

Applies to: All employees

Booking time off

Where do I see how many days I have left?

In Workday, under Absence. Your balance updates overnight after a request is approved. The HR

service desk cannot look this up faster than you can, so check Workday first.

How far ahead do I need to ask?

Three weeks for five consecutive days or more. Less than five days needs no minimum notice,

but tell your team.

Can my manager say no?

Yes, where business needs require it. You are entitled to an explanation and to propose other

dates. If you think a refusal is unreasonable, raise it with People Operations.

What happens to days I do not use?

You may carry 5 days into the next year, and they must be used by 31 March. Anything above 5

days is lost. Unused days are only paid out when you leave.

I am ill during my holiday. Do I lose the days?

No, if you provide a medical certificate. The days are reclassified as sick leave and

returned to your balance.

Do public holidays come out of my allowance?

No. Public holidays are in addition to your 25 days.

I work part time. How much do I get?

Pro-rated. A 0.6 FTE employee receives 15 days plus pro-rated public holidays.

Can I take unpaid leave?

Up to 20 days a year by agreement with your manager. Longer breaks are covered by the Unpaid

Leave and Career Breaks policy (NW-POL-017).
