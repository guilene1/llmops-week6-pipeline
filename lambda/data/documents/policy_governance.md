# Policy governance

## Document identifiers

Every policy has an ID of the form NW-POL-nnn. The ID never changes, even when the policy is
rewritten. The version number changes instead.

## Versions

Versions are written as year.revision. Version 2026.2 is the second revision issued in 2026.
The effective date at the top of a policy is the date it takes effect, which may be later
than the date it was published.

## Superseded documents

When a policy is replaced, the old version is marked SUPERSEDED and kept in the library. It
is retained for seven years because questions about what the rules were at a past date do
come up, particularly in disputes and audits.

A superseded document is never the answer to what is the policy. If you find yourself reading
one, look for the version that replaced it - the replacement is named at the top.

## Review cycle

Every policy is reviewed each January by its owner. Policies that have not been reviewed for
two cycles are withdrawn.

## Who owns what

- People Operations owns employment, pay, leave and performance policies
- Legal and Compliance owns conduct, ethics, privacy and regulatory policies
- IT Security owns acceptable use, device and data classification policies
- Facilities owns health, safety and building policies
