# Moving for work

This policy covers relocation Northwind asks you to make. It does not cover moving because
you want to, which is dealt with in the Remote and Hybrid Working Policy.

## What is provided

| | Within a country | International |
|---|---|---|
| Removal of household goods | Up to 8,000 USD | Up to 20,000 USD |
| Temporary accommodation | Up to 30 days | Up to 90 days |
| Flights for you and household | Economy | Economy |
| Immigration and legal fees | n/a | Paid in full |
| Settling-in allowance | 3,000 USD | 6,000 USD |
| Tax advice in both countries | n/a | Paid, one year |

The settling-in allowance is paid with your first salary in the new location and is intended
for deposits, school registration, utilities and the many small costs a move creates. No
receipts are required and it is taxable in most locations.

## What is not provided

Northwind does not buy your house, cover a loss on a sale, or pay a mortgage
differential. Storage beyond 90 days is not covered either.

## Immigration

Northwind's immigration adviser handles the application and Northwind pays every fee,
including for your dependants. Do not start work in the new country before the adviser has
confirmed you may.

Timelines vary from six weeks to nine months depending on the country. Do not give notice on
housing or schooling until the permit is issued.

## Repayment

If you resign within 12 months of relocating, a proportion of the cost is repayable: 100
percent within six months, 50 percent between six and twelve months. Nothing is repayable if
Northwind ends your employment, or if you move again at Northwind's request.

This is set out in the relocation agreement you sign before anything is booked.

## Family

Northwind pays for immigration for your spouse or partner and dependent children, and for
their flights. Support with schooling and partner employment is arranged through the
relocation provider but not funded.

## Tax

International moves have tax consequences in both countries, sometimes for several years.
Northwind pays for professional advice for the first year and you should take it before you
move, not after.
