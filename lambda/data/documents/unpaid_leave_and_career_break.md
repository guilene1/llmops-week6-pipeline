# Unpaid leave

## Short unpaid leave

Up to 20 working days of unpaid leave per calendar year may be agreed with your manager, for
any reason. It does not need People Operations approval. Benefits continue and paid time off
continues to accrue.

## Career break

Employees with at least 2 years of continuous service may apply for a career break of between
3 and 12 months. Applications go to your manager and to People Operations at least 3 months
before the intended start date.

During a career break:

- Employment continues but salary stops
- Medical cover continues for the first 3 months, then may be continued at your own cost
- Pension and retirement contributions stop
- Paid time off does not accrue
- Your role is held open for breaks of up to 6 months. For longer breaks Northwind commits to
  a role at the same level, which may not be the same role

Career breaks are approved at the discretion of the business and are not an entitlement. A
declined application must be explained in writing.

Only one career break may be taken in any 5-year period. Northwind does not offer paid
sabbaticals.
