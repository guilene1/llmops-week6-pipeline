# What we expect

## Communication

Default to written and asynchronous. Decisions taken in a meeting are written up in the
relevant channel within one working day so that people in other time zones are not excluded.

Response expectations: Slack within one working day, email within two working days. Neither
is expected outside your working hours, and nobody should feel obliged to reply at night or
at the weekend.

## Meetings

Every meeting needs an agenda. A meeting without one can be declined without explanation.
Meetings default to 25 or 50 minutes so that people have a gap between them.

No-meeting Wednesdays apply to engineering and design. Other teams may adopt this.

## Attendance

Tell your manager and your team if you will be away from work during your normal hours.
Unexplained absence of three consecutive working days without contact is treated as a
disciplinary matter under the Absence Management Procedure (NW-POL-014).

## Use of company property

Laptops, phones and accounts are provided for work. Reasonable personal use is fine.
Do not use company accounts to run a personal business.
