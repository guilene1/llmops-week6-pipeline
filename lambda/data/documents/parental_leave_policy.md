# Parental Leave Policy

## Eligibility

Employees with at least 12 months of continuous service on the expected date of
birth or placement are eligible for paid parental leave. Employees with less than
12 months of service are eligible for unpaid leave and job protection under
applicable law.

## Paid leave entitlement

The birthing parent receives 16 weeks of paid leave at 100 percent of base
salary. The non-birthing parent, including adoptive and foster parents, receives
8 weeks of paid leave at 100 percent of base salary.

Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement date.

## Taking leave in blocks

Leave may be taken in up to 3 separate blocks. Each block must be at least 2
weeks long. Block dates must be agreed with your manager at least 30 days before
the first day of each block.

## Notice

Tell your manager and People Operations at least 60 days before your intended
start date where possible. Notice of less than 30 days may delay the start of
paid leave by up to 2 weeks while cover is arranged.

## Return to work

You return to the same role at the same level and pay. If that role no longer
exists, Northwind offers a comparable role at the same level and pay.

For the first 4 weeks after returning, you may work a reduced schedule of 80
percent at full pay. This must be agreed with your manager before your return
date.

## Interaction with other leave

Parental leave does not reduce annual leave entitlement, and annual leave
continues to accrue during paid parental leave. Sick leave may not be used to
extend parental leave.
