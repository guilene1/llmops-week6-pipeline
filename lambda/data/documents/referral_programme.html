Title: Employee Referral Programme

Document ID: NW-POL-038

Version: 2026.1

Effective: 2026-01-01

Owner: Talent Acquisition

Applies to: All employees except hiring managers for their own vacancies

Refer someone you would want to work with

Most of the best people at Northwind arrived because somebody here recommended them. The

programme pays you for doing that.

The bonus

2,000 US dollars, or the local equivalent, when someone you referred is hired and completes

90 days of employment.

The bonus is 3,000 US dollars for roles marked as hard to fill on the careers page. Which

roles those are changes; the page is the authority, not this document.

Payment is made with the salary run after the 90 day point. It is taxable and paid through

payroll.

How to refer someone

Submit the referral in Workday under Referrals before the candidate applies, or within two

days of them applying. Include how you know them and why you think they would do well here.

The referral is valid for 12 months. If your candidate is not hired now but is hired for

another role within that year, the bonus is still yours.

Who can refer

Any employee, except the hiring manager for that vacancy, anyone on the interview panel, and

anyone in Talent Acquisition. Members of the executive team do not receive referral bonuses.

If two people refer the same candidate, the earlier submission counts.

What a referral is not

A referral is a recommendation, not a decision. Referred candidates go through the same

interview process, with the same scorecards, as everybody else. You will not be told the

details of how they did, and you should not ask the panel.

Please do not refer someone without telling them. It is an unpleasant surprise to be contacted

about a job you did not apply for.

Family and close relationships

You may refer a family member or partner, but you must declare the relationship in the

referral and you cannot be involved in the hiring process in any way. If they are hired, you

will not be in the same reporting line, in either direction. See the conflicts of interest

section of the Code of Conduct.

Boomerangs

Former employees may be referred and the bonus applies, unless they left less than six months

ago.
