# Parental leave, United States

This policy applies to employees of the United States entity. Employees in the United
Kingdom, Germany and India are covered by their own national policies, which differ.

## Entitlement

A primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of leave at full pay. A secondary caregiver receives
6 weeks at full pay. Both apply to birth, adoption and surrogacy, and to any employee
regardless of gender.

There is no minimum service requirement. Leave may start up to 4 weeks before the expected
date of birth or placement.

## Taking the leave

Leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the 12 months following birth or
placement. Each block must be at least two weeks long.

Tell your manager and People Operations at least 30 days before you intend to start, or as
soon as reasonably possible where the date was not predictable.

## Relationship to FMLA

Northwind paid parental leave runs concurrently with any entitlement under the Family and
Medical Leave Act. It does not add to it. FMLA provides up to 12 weeks of job-protected
unpaid leave; Northwind pays you for 16 or 6 weeks of that period.

## Benefits during leave

Medical, dental, vision and life cover continue unchanged. Your 401(k) contributions continue
from your paid leave, with the employer match unaffected. Paid time off continues to accrue.

## Returning

You return to the same role. If you want to change your hours on return, discuss it with your
manager at least 4 weeks before your return date. Requests for a temporary reduction in hours
for up to 6 months after return are normally approved.
