Jury service and public duties

Northwind pays your normal salary for the full duration of jury service. There is no cap on
the number of days and it does not come out of your paid time off.

Where the court pays a loss of earnings allowance, claim it and pass it to payroll, who will
deduct the amount from your salary for that period. Do not claim the allowance and keep it as
well as full pay.

Send your summons to hr-help@northwind.example as soon as you receive it so cover can be
arranged.

Northwind does not ask employees to seek deferral or excusal from jury service.

Paid time off is also provided for:
  - service as a magistrate, school governor or local councillor, up to 10 days per year
  - reserve forces annual training, up to 15 days per year
  - attendance as a witness under subpoena, for the days required

Voting time off is provided where the law requires it and where polling hours do not allow
voting outside working hours.
