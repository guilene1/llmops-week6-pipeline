# Where you work

Northwind is hybrid by default. Most roles are based at an office and worked partly from home.
Some roles are fully remote, and a few must be on site.

## The default: hybrid

Employees attached to an office are expected in it **eight days a month**, arranged with the
team rather than fixed centrally. Teams choose their own anchor days and publish them.

Eight days is a floor for the team's benefit, not a attendance metric. Nobody counts your
badge swipes, and no manager should.

## Fully remote roles

Some roles are advertised as fully remote and have no office expectation. That is set when the
role is created, and it is written in your offer letter.

An existing hybrid role does not become remote because you move. If you want to relocate, read
the section below before making any commitment.

## Roles that must be on site

Facilities, some IT hardware work, and anyone whose work requires physical access to the
Bangalore data centre area. These roles say so in the job description.

## Core hours

10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone, when you are expected to be reachable. Outside those
hours, work when it suits you, provided your commitments are met.

Teams spanning more than six time zones agree a narrower overlap and write it down.

## Working from another country

This is the part people get wrong, and it is the one with real consequences.

Working from a country other than the one that employs you can create a tax liability for you
and a permanent establishment risk for Northwind, sometimes after as little as ten days.
Immigration rules may also prohibit working on a tourist entry.

So: tell People Operations **at least four weeks** before working from another country, even
for a week, even on holiday. Most requests are approved. What is not acceptable is finding out
afterwards.

Northwind allows up to 20 working days a year from another country, subject to that approval.
Anything longer needs a formal international assignment, which takes months.

## Changing your arrangement

Ask your manager. Requests to change your working pattern are considered on whether the role
can be done that way, not on whether somebody else was allowed to. If declined, you are
entitled to a reason in writing.

Employees in the United Kingdom have a statutory right to request flexible working from day
one, and Northwind responds within two months.

## Equipment and expenses

See Equipment and Home Working Allowance (NW-POL-037). Northwind does not reimburse home
internet or coworking memberships, except where a coworking space replaces an office that
Northwind has closed.
