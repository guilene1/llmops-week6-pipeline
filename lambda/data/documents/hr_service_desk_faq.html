Title: HR Service Desk Frequently Asked Questions

Document ID: NW-POL-007

Version: 2026.1

Effective: 2026-01-01

Owner: People Operations

Applies to: All employees

HR service desk

How do I contact HR?

Email hr-help@northwind.example. The service desk is staffed Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00

in your local office time zone. Urgent payroll problems go to payroll@northwind.example.

How quickly will I get an answer?

Routine questions are answered within two working days. Requests that need a decision from a

manager or from Legal take longer and you will be told who it is with.

Where do I find my payslip?

In Workday, under Pay. Payslips are available from the morning of pay day and are kept for

seven years.

How do I change my bank details?

In Workday, under Pay, then Payment Elections. Changes made after the 20th of the month take

effect the following month. Northwind will never ask you to send bank details by email.

How do I book time off?

In Workday, under Absence. Your manager approves it. See the Time Off Policy (NW-POL-010).

How do I get a letter confirming my employment?

Request it from the service desk. Standard employment verification letters are issued within

three working days. Letters for mortgage or visa purposes that need specific wording take up

to five.

Who do I talk to about a problem with my manager?

People Operations, or the anonymous ethics line at ethics.northwind.example. You are not

required to raise it with your manager first.
