# Booking a trip

## Before you book

Every trip needs your manager's approval in Workday, with the purpose and the estimated cost.
Approve the trip before booking anything, not afterwards.

Ask whether the trip is necessary. A two-day visit that could be a video call costs about
1,500 dollars and two days of somebody's life. Some trips are clearly worth it: onboarding a
new team member, a customer at risk, a workshop that needs a whiteboard.

## How to book

Through the corporate travel tool, linked in Workday. Booking outside the tool is allowed only
where the tool cannot make the booking, and then you claim it back on expenses.

The tool applies the limits in the Expense and Reimbursement Policy (NW-POL-040) and the Per
Diem Rates (NW-POL-042) automatically, so a booking inside policy needs no further approval.

## Flights

Economy under six hours. Premium economy over six hours. Business class needs director
approval and is normally for flights over ten hours where you work the following day.

Book at least 14 days ahead where the trip is predictable.

## Hotels

Within the cap for the city in the per diem table. Where a conference hotel is above the cap
and staying elsewhere would be impractical, book it and note the reason; it will be approved.

## Trains and cars

Rail rather than flying where the door-to-door journey is under five hours. Standard class
under three hours, first class above it where it is not more expensive than flying.

Hire cars are compact or intermediate. Northwind does not reimburse insurance excess waivers
that duplicate the corporate policy; check the travel tool before buying cover at the desk.

## Personal travel attached to a business trip

Allowed, and you do not need permission. Northwind pays what the business-only trip would
have cost and you pay the difference. Send the comparison quote with your claim.

Northwind's travel insurance does not cover the personal portion. Arrange your own.

## Travel insurance and safety

All business travel is covered by Northwind's policy. The card and the emergency number are
in Workday; save them to your phone before you go.

Check the destination guidance before booking travel outside your usual country. Trips to
locations with a government advisory against travel need executive approval.

## Visas

Northwind pays for visas, permits and any vaccination required for business travel, and the
time spent obtaining them is working time.
