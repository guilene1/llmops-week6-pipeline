# Retirement provision

Northwind contributes to a retirement arrangement for every employee, in the form their
country provides. The arrangements are not equivalent to each other and cannot be compared
line by line, because the tax treatment and the state provision behind them differ.

## United States: 401(k)

Northwind matches 100 percent of the first 4 percent of eligible pay that you contribute. If
you contribute 4 percent or more, Northwind contributes 4 percent. If you contribute 2
percent, Northwind contributes 2 percent.

The match vests immediately. There is no cliff and no graded schedule: money Northwind puts
in is yours from the day it arrives.

You may contribute pre-tax, Roth, or a mix, up to the annual limit set by the Internal
Revenue Service. Employees aged 50 and over may make catch-up contributions above that limit.

Auto-enrolment applies at 4 percent from your second month, so you receive the full match
without doing anything. You may change or stop contributions at any time in Workday.

Investment options, fund performance and fees are in the plan documents. Northwind does not
give investment advice, and nobody at Northwind may tell you which fund to choose.

## United Kingdom: workplace pension

Northwind contributes 8 percent of qualifying earnings whether or not you contribute
yourself. Most employers contribute 3 percent, which is the statutory minimum; ours is
higher, and it is not conditional on your own contribution.

You are auto-enrolled at a 4 percent employee contribution and may opt out within one month
for a full refund, or reduce it at any time. Salary sacrifice is available and reduces your
National Insurance as well as your income tax.

## Germany: statutory plus company pension

The statutory scheme (gesetzliche Rentenversicherung) is funded by equal employee and
employer contributions at the rate set each year.

On top of that, Northwind pays 4 percent of salary into a company pension
(Direktversicherung). Employees may make additional contributions through deferred
compensation (Entgeltumwandlung), and Northwind adds the statutory 15 percent subsidy on
those.

## India: Provident Fund and gratuity

Northwind contributes to the Employees Provident Fund at the statutory rate of 12 percent of
basic salary. Gratuity is paid as required by the Payment of Gratuity Act on leaving after
five years of continuous service.

## If you leave

United States: your balance including the match is yours. You may leave it in the plan, roll
it over, or withdraw it, with tax consequences for the last of those.

United Kingdom and Germany: the pension pot remains yours and moves with you. Contact the
provider, not Northwind, once your employment has ended.

India: Provident Fund balances transfer to your next employer using your Universal Account
Number.

## What Northwind will not do

Nobody at Northwind, including People Operations, may recommend a fund, project a retirement
income, or advise on whether to opt out. These are regulated activities. The plan providers
offer guidance services, and they are listed in Workday under Benefits.
