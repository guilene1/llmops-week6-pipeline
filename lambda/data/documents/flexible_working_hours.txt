Title: Flexible Working Hours
Document ID: NW-POL-052
Version: 2026.1
Effective: 2026-01-01
Owner: People Operations
Applies to: All employees

Flexible hours

Standard hours are 40 a week in the United States and India, 37.5 in the United Kingdom, and
38 in Germany. Within those, when you work is largely up to you.

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Be reachable then. Outside them,
start early, finish late, take a long lunch, split your day around the school run.

You do not need permission for any of that. You do need to tell your team what to expect, and
to keep your calendar honest.

Compressed hours, for example four longer days instead of five, are available by agreement
with your manager. They are a contract change, so People Operations writes it up.

Part-time arrangements are available in every role where the work allows. Pay, paid time off
and benefits are pro-rated. A 0.6 FTE employee receives 15 days of paid time off rather than
25.

Term-time working is available in the United Kingdom and Germany, by agreement.

Nothing here changes the total hours you are contracted to work, or the expectation that you
are contactable during core hours. If flexibility is being used to work substantially fewer
hours than contracted, that is a performance conversation, and it should be had plainly rather
than through calendar policing.

Employees in the United Kingdom have a statutory right to request a permanent change to their
working pattern from their first day. Northwind answers within two months, in writing, with
reasons if the answer is no.
