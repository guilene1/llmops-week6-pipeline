# Cover if something serious happens

Two benefits that nobody thinks about until they matter. Both are provided at no cost to you
and require no medical examination at the standard level of cover.

## Life assurance

Northwind provides life cover of four times your annual base salary, payable to the people
you nominate, if you die while employed.

Nominate your beneficiaries in Workday under Benefits. If you nominate nobody, the payment is
made at the discretion of the scheme trustees, which takes longer and may not go where you
would have chosen. Review your nomination after any change in your family circumstances.

Cover above the scheme limit, which varies by country, may require medical underwriting. The
scheme administrator will contact you if that applies to your salary.

Bonus and commission are not included in the calculation. Base salary only.

## Income protection

If you are unable to work because of illness or injury for more than 13 continuous weeks,
income protection pays 60 percent of your base salary until you return to work, reach
retirement age, or the policy ends.

The 13 week period runs alongside company sick pay, not after it. In the United Kingdom,
where company sick pay is eight weeks at full salary, there is a five week gap covered by
Statutory Sick Pay before income protection begins. Plan for that gap.

Payments are made through payroll and are taxed as employment income.

## Making a claim

People Operations starts the claim for income protection, usually in week eight of absence,
so that there is time for the insurer to assess it before payments are due. You do not need
to do this yourself, but you will be asked to consent to the insurer contacting your doctor.

For life assurance, the family or executor contacts the HR service desk. Northwind notifies
the insurer and supports the claim. There is nothing for the family to pay.

## Critical illness

Northwind does not provide critical illness cover. Some employees choose to buy it
individually, and the benefits provider offers a group discount. This is not a Northwind
benefit and Northwind does not contribute to it.

## While you are on leave

Both benefits continue unchanged during parental leave, sick leave and any other paid leave.
During an unpaid career break, life assurance continues for the first three months and then
lapses unless you arrange to pay the premium.
