# Parental leave, Germany

This policy applies to employees of the German entity and sits alongside the statutory
Mutterschutz and Elternzeit framework. The United States and United Kingdom policies do not
apply here.

## Maternity protection (Mutterschutz)

Statutory maternity protection runs 6 weeks before and 8 weeks after birth, extended to 12
weeks after birth for premature or multiple births. Pay during this period is the
Mutterschaftsgeld allowance topped up by Northwind to 100 percent of net salary.

## Parental leave (Elternzeit)

Elternzeit of up to 3 years per child is available to either parent, of which up to 24 months
may be taken between the child third and eighth birthday. Elternzeit is unpaid by the
employer; Elterngeld is paid by the state.

Northwind adds a supplement of 8 weeks at full pay for the parent taking the first period of
Elternzeit, payable within the first 12 months after birth.

Written notice of Elternzeit must be given at least 7 weeks before it starts, and must state
the periods to be taken within the first 2 years.

## Works council

Any change to this policy requires consultation with the works council before it takes effect.

## Returning

You have a statutory right to return to your role. Part-time work during Elternzeit of
between 15 and 32 hours per week is possible by agreement and is normally approved.
