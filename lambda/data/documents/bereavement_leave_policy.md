# Bereavement leave

Northwind provides paid bereavement leave. You do not need to provide documentation and you
do not need to use paid time off.

## Entitlement

- 10 working days on the death of a partner, child, or pregnancy loss at any stage
- 5 working days on the death of a parent, sibling, grandparent or grandchild
- 3 working days on the death of another close family member or a close friend
- 1 working day to attend a funeral in any other case

These are minimums. If you need more time, ask. Managers are expected to agree additional
paid or unpaid leave where it is needed, and to involve People Operations rather than decline
a request themselves.

## Taking the leave

Tell your manager. The absence is recorded in Workday as Bereavement so that it does not come
out of your paid time off. Leave does not have to be taken consecutively and can be taken
within 12 months of the death.

## Support

The Employee Assistance Programme (NW-POL-031) provides free confidential counselling to you
and your immediate household, including bereavement support.
