# Performance Improvement Plan Guide for Managers

## When to open a plan

Open a Performance Improvement Plan when performance has been below expectations
for at least one review cycle and informal coaching has not closed the gap. Do not
open a plan for conduct issues; those follow the disciplinary route.

Talk to your HR Business Partner before telling the employee. Opening a plan
without HR involvement is not permitted.

## Duration

Standard duration is 60 calendar days. A 30 day plan may be used for a clearly
scoped and measurable gap. Extension beyond 90 days is not permitted; at that
point the plan closes with a decision.

## What the plan must contain

- The specific gap, described in observable behaviour, not personality
- Two to four measurable objectives with dates
- The support Northwind will provide, including time and training
- Review meeting dates, at least every 2 weeks
- The consequence if objectives are not met, stated plainly

## Review meetings

Hold every scheduled review meeting even when progress is good. Record what was
discussed within 2 business days and share the record with the employee. A missed
review meeting weakens the plan and may invalidate a later dismissal.

## Outcomes

Three outcomes are possible: the plan is met and closed, the plan is extended
once within the 90 day limit, or the plan is not met and the case moves to the
Offboarding and Termination Procedure owned by People Operations.

## Pay and promotion during a plan

An employee on an active plan is not eligible for a merit increase, a promotion,
or an internal move until the plan closes successfully.

## Confidentiality

The existence of a plan is shared only with the employee, the manager, the HR
Business Partner, and the department head. Do not discuss it with the wider team.
