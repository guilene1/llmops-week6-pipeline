IT ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
========================


ACCOUNTS AND PASSWORDS

Every employee receives a named account. Shared accounts are not permitted.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is required
on all company systems and cannot be disabled.

Report a suspected account compromise to the IT service desk immediately, and in
any case within 1 hour of noticing it.


COMPANY DEVICES

Company laptops are managed and encrypted. Do not install software from outside
the approved catalogue without an IT exception. Personal use of a company laptop
is permitted within reason and does not create a right to privacy on that device.


PERSONAL DEVICES

Personal phones may access company email and chat through the managed app
container. Personal laptops may not access company systems except through the
virtual desktop.


APPROVED AI TOOLS

Only AI tools listed in the approved catalogue may be used with company data. As
of this version the approved list is the internal assistant and the approved
enterprise coding assistant.

Do not paste customer data, employee personal data, unreleased financial
information, or source code into any tool that is not on the approved list.


DATA CLASSIFICATION

Four levels are used: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Employee
personal data and compensation data are Restricted. Restricted data may only be
stored in systems approved for that level.


EMAIL AND PHISHING

Report suspected phishing with the Report Phish button. Do not forward suspicious
messages to colleagues. IT runs simulated phishing tests quarterly.


LEAVING THE COMPANY

All company equipment must be returned by your last working day. Access is
revoked at 17:00 on your last working day, or earlier if agreed.
