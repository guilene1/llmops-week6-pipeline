# Confidential support, free to use

The Employee Assistance Programme is a service run by an outside provider, not by Northwind.
It gives you and anyone in your household free, confidential support on almost anything.

## What it covers

- Counselling for stress, anxiety, low mood, grief and relationship difficulties
- Practical advice on debt, budgeting, housing and consumer problems
- Legal information on family, employment and neighbour matters
- Support for carers, and for parents of children with additional needs
- Help finding childcare or eldercare
- Bereavement support, including after a pregnancy loss

Counselling is available by phone, video or in person, usually within five working days and
sooner in an emergency. The standard allocation is eight sessions per issue per year, and the
provider will say if more are needed and arrange them.

## How to use it

Telephone 0800 numbers for each country are in Workday under Benefits, along with the app and
the website. The service is staffed 24 hours a day, every day.

You do not tell Northwind, and you do not need permission. There is no referral, no manager
approval, and no record in your personnel file.

## What Northwind sees

Northwind receives a quarterly report showing how many people used the service and the broad
categories of issue, with no names and no details. Where a team is small enough that numbers
could identify someone, they are merged into a larger group before reporting.

Your manager will never be told that you called, what you discussed, or that you are
receiving counselling.

The single exception is a serious and immediate risk to life, where the provider may act as
any clinician would. That has nothing to do with your employment.

## If you are worried about someone else

You may call the programme about a colleague you are concerned about and ask for advice on
how to help. You do not need their consent to do that, and you do not have to name them.

## What it is not

The programme is not a substitute for your doctor, and it does not prescribe. It is also not
the route for raising a complaint about work: harassment, discrimination or a problem with
your manager should go to People Operations or the ethics line, which are covered by their own
policies. The counsellor can help you think it through, but cannot act on it.
