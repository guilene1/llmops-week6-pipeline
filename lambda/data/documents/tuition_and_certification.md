# Paying for learning

Northwind reimburses up to 5,000 US dollars per calendar year, or the local equivalent, for
study that is relevant to your work now or to a role you could reasonably move into at
Northwind.

## What qualifies

- Professional certifications and the exams for them
- University and college courses, including part-time degrees
- Conference attendance where you will present or report back
- Online course subscriptions, where you complete the course
- Professional body membership where it is required or clearly useful
- Books and study materials for an approved course

"Relevant" is interpreted broadly. A support engineer studying for a cloud certification
qualifies. So does a designer taking a course in accessibility law, or a manager studying
coaching. A course with no connection to your work or any Northwind role does not.

## What does not qualify

Travel and accommodation for a conference come out of the travel budget, not this allowance.
Courses your team has already bought a licence for are not reimbursed separately. Preparation
for a career change away from Northwind is not covered, though nobody will interrogate you
about your motives.

## Approval

Your manager approves anything up to 1,500 dollars. Above that, your manager and their
manager both approve. Approval is needed before you enrol, not after, and the request goes
through Workday with a link to the course and its cost.

A declined request must be explained in writing. "No budget" is an explanation; silence is not.

## Payment and exams

Northwind pays the provider directly where possible, so you are not out of pocket. Where you
must pay, claim it back on expenses with the receipt and evidence of enrolment.

Exam fees are covered for the first sitting and one retake. A third attempt is at your own
cost. Northwind does not reimburse a certification you already hold and are renewing, unless
renewal requires further study.

## Study time

You may use up to five working days a year of paid study leave for an approved course, agreed
with your manager in advance. Exam days are in addition and do not come out of that allowance
or out of your paid time off.

## If you leave

If Northwind spends more than 2,000 dollars on a single course or certification and you
resign within 12 months of completing it, a proportion is repayable: 100 percent within six
months, 50 percent between six and twelve. This is set out in the approval you sign before
enrolling, and it does not apply if Northwind ends your employment.

Nothing is repayable for amounts under 2,000 dollars, and nothing is repayable for
professional body membership.
