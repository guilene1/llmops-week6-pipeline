# When you are paid, and how to read it

## Pay dates

| Entity | Frequency | Paid on |
|---|---|---|
| United States | Semi-monthly | 15th and the last working day |
| United Kingdom | Monthly | Last working day |
| Germany | Monthly | Last working day |
| India | Monthly | Last working day |

Where a pay date falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the working day
before, never after.

## Payslips

Available in Workday under Pay from the morning of pay day. Payslips are kept for seven years
and you can download them at any time, including after you leave, for six months.

Northwind does not post paper payslips.

## Changing your bank details

In Workday, under Pay, then Payment Elections. Changes made after the 20th of the month take
effect the following month, because the payroll file is prepared then.

Northwind will never ask you to send bank details by email, and will never phone to ask you
to change them. If somebody does, it is fraud. Report it to payroll@northwind.example.

## Overtime

In the United States, employees classified as non-exempt under the Fair Labor Standards Act
are paid 1.5 times their hourly rate for hours over 40 in a week. Whether a role is exempt
depends on its duties and salary, not on its title, and the classification is shown on your
offer letter.

This classification does not exist in the United Kingdom, German or Indian entities. In those
locations, salaried employees are not paid overtime; where work regularly exceeds contracted
hours, that is a workload conversation with your manager, not a payroll one.

Employees on shift patterns in any location receive the shift premiums in their contract.

## Bonus

Where your offer includes a bonus target, it is expressed as a percentage of base salary, and
paid in March for the previous calendar year. It is discretionary and depends on both company
and individual performance. You must be employed on the payment date to receive it.

## Salary reviews

Salaries are reviewed once a year, effective 1 April. A review is not a guarantee of an
increase. Promotions may take effect at any time and are not held for the review cycle.

## Deductions you may see

Tax and social contributions, pension or retirement, and anything you elected in benefits.
Court-ordered deductions, where they apply, appear as a single line and Finance will have
written to you before the first one.

## Getting a problem fixed

payroll@northwind.example, and mark it urgent if you have been underpaid. Underpayments are
corrected by an off-cycle payment within three working days, not held to the next run.
