# Headcount Plan 2026

## Summary

Approved net headcount growth for 2026 is 84 roles, taking total headcount from
612 to 696 by 31 December 2026. The plan assumes an attrition rate of 11 percent.

## Approved additions by department

- Engineering: 41 roles, weighted to the first and second quarters
- Sales: 18 roles, of which 12 are quota-carrying
- Customer Success: 11 roles
- People Operations: 4 roles
- Finance: 3 roles
- Marketing: 7 roles

## Hiring pace

No more than 40 percent of the annual plan may be opened in the first quarter.
Requisitions beyond that require Chief Executive approval, regardless of budget
availability.

## Backfills

Backfills for voluntary departures are pre-approved at L1 to L5 and do not consume
plan slots. Backfills at L6 and above require executive sponsor approval and are
reviewed monthly.

## Contractor conversion

Up to 12 contractor conversions are included in the Engineering number. Each
conversion consumes one plan slot.

## Contingency and freeze triggers

A contingency pool of 6 unallocated roles is held by Finance and released
quarterly. If bookings fall more than 15 percent below plan at the end of any
quarter, new requisitions pause automatically until the next executive review.

## Restructuring provision

The plan includes a provision for a targeted reduction of up to 15 roles in the
second half of the year if the platform consolidation programme completes on
schedule. No communication on this provision is authorised before the executive
review in July.
