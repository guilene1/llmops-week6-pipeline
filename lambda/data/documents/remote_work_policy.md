# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Hybrid baseline

Northwind operates a hybrid model. Employees assigned to an office location are
expected on site 2 days per week. Teams choose their own anchor days and publish
them in the team calendar.

## Fully remote roles

Some roles are designated fully remote at the point of hire. The designation is
recorded in your offer letter. A hybrid role cannot be converted to fully remote
without approval from your department head and People Operations.

## Working from another location

You may work from a different location within your country of employment for up
to 30 calendar days per year without approval.

Working from another country requires approval at least 30 days in advance,
because it creates tax and employment obligations for Northwind. Maximum duration
is 60 days per calendar year. Approval is not granted for countries where
Northwind has no legal entity and no employer of record arrangement.

## Home office equipment

Northwind provides a laptop, an external monitor, a keyboard and a mouse. A
one-time home office allowance of 500 dollars is available after 90 days of
service. Claim it through the expense system under category HOME-OFFICE.
Furniture is covered by this allowance and is not reimbursed separately.

## Internet and phone

Fully remote employees receive 60 dollars per month toward internet costs. Hybrid
employees do not receive this allowance. No receipts are required, and the
allowance is paid with your regular salary.

## Availability

Remote work does not change core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local
time. If you need to be unreachable during core hours, record it in your calendar
and tell your team.
